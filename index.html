Should Do

A task app that tells you what to do next, instead of handing you a long list.

Should Do suggests one task that fits the time you have. If you allow it, it looks at your calendar to find free time, so the suggestion is something you can actually do now.

What it does

  • One suggestion at a time. Up Next shows the task that fits your next free gap.
  • Daily Planner. Groups today's tasks into the free time in your calendar.
  • Quick capture. Write a task down in seconds and add details later.
  • Do-after dates and repeats. Tasks stay out of sight until you can start them, and can repeat on a schedule or after you finish them.
  • Widgets. Capture a task, see the next one, or complete one from your Home Screen.

Private by design

Your tasks stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no tracking and no advertising. Calendar access is optional and read-only. Read the privacy policy.

Should Do is in testing and is not yet on the App Store. Questions or feedback? See support.
