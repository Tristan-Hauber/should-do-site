Support

For help, questions or feedback about Should Do, email tristan.shoulddo@gmail.com.

When you write

It helps to include:

  • what you were doing and what happened instead;
  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • whether you are using a TestFlight test version, and its build number (shown in the TestFlight app).

If you are testing through TestFlight, you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app, including a screenshot.

Common questions

Does Should Do change my calendar?

No. It only reads events to find your free time, and only if you allow it. You can stop it at any time in Settings > Apple Calendar in the app, or withdraw permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Calendars.

Where are my tasks stored?

On your iPhone. They are not sent to the developer. See the privacy policy for details, including backups.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. Copies in your own iCloud or device backups follow your Apple settings.
